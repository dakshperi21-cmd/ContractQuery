Everything on the site, in one place.

Probable is a free tool for looking up Polymarket prediction-market contracts and understanding what's actually driving their price — built for people with no finance background.

Market search

Search any Polymarket contract by keyword — "Fed rate decision," "Bitcoin $150k," a candidate's name — and get its live odds, volume, and liquidity, without needing a Polymarket URL.

Market browser

Don't know what to search for yet? The homepage shows a curated, categorized feed of what people are actually trading right now — Politics & Elections, Fed & Economy, Tech & Crypto, Sports — click straight in.

Price history

Every contract page includes an interactive chart of how its odds have moved over time, so you can see the exact moment the market reacted to news — not just read about it.

ELI5 mode

Flip on ELI5 ("Explain Like I'm 5") and jargon like Liquidity or Spread gets a plain-language, hover-over explanation with a real-world analogy — plus a note connecting that specific market's numbers back to a core economics idea: supply & demand, risk vs. reward, opportunity cost, or how prices aggregate information.

Glossary

A standing, browsable reference of every term used on the site — each with a plain-language definition and an analogy, tagged back to the concept it illustrates.

Starter questions

Every contract page ends with a few discussion prompts — "What would have to happen for this to flip?" — meant to be talked through with a friend, a teacher, or a class, not just read.

Free accounts & recents

Create a free account and your searches and the contracts you open are automatically saved to a Recents list, so you can pick up right where you left off.

Built for classrooms and self-learners

Every feature here — the browser, ELI5 mode, the glossary, starter questions — exists for the same reason: financial literacy is usually taught with hypotheticals, but prediction markets put a real, constantly-updating number in front of you. Probable's goal is to make that number legible to anyone, not just people who already know the vocabulary.
